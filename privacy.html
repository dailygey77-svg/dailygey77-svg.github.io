Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

DAILY Uploader is used only by the owner of the DAILY YouTube channels, on the owner's own computer.

  • The app accesses YouTube only through the YouTube API Services, to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channels, schedule them and read the state of those uploads.
  • The Google access token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any other service.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users, and does not sell or share any data.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token is then useless and can be deleted.

By using this app you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
